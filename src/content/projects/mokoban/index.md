Mokoban is a Sokoban-inspired puzzle game for Android that I designed and programmed on my own in three days.

The game follows the classic Sokoban idea: you move around a small grid and push the blocks onto their target tiles. Blocks can only be pushed, never pulled, so one careless move can lock the whole level.


With only three days, scope was the main design decision. I kept the rule set small and focused on getting one complete loop working from start to finish: load a level, play it, win it, and move on to the next one. 

Mokoban is a small game, but it showed me how much I can finish alone when the scope is clear, and it gave me a first look at what puzzle level design and mobile development ask for.
